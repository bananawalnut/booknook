## Current reading desk

Book and notebook remain visible side by side, with independent scrolling, a resizable divider and one notebook-left/right button. The desk uses matching warm paper and green ink. Notes are distinct text entries separated by quiet rules. Each has a light margin for adding a character card, note options and flipping to its own drawing board. Finger or Pencil draws on the iPad board; two fingers pan and pinch zooms. The board expands around a pan/zoom viewport; returning to text preserves prose, original ink, creation context and note order. Add note starts another entry. Original handwriting and reviewed transcription remain independently recoverable.

Completed notebook and reader ink is checkpointed to a private local journal before asynchronous final persistence. Acknowledged captures recover after process termination, including unresolved reader placement and failed saves. Recovery retains original bytes, source context, parents and retry IDs. Ambiguous reader marks need an explicit retry or unplaced save before portable export. Transcription can be canceled; late recognition results cannot replace a canceled or edited draft. First meaningful text/drawing input captures its available reading context before asynchronous saving; an automatically opened empty note does not invent a creation time. Drawing captures retain their own original time and ebook/audio locations through retry and recovery.
